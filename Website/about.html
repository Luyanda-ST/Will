Our story

Why we game.

Because playing games brings people together, competition pushes us forward, and every player deserves a world-class arena.

Est. 2023 · Johannesburg

A bold idea, built for the city's players.

Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena was founded in 2023 by lifelong gamer and entrepreneur Jason Naidoo. His goal was simple: create the gaming venue Johannesburg had been waiting for.

Today, our arena is a home for casual players, growing teams and established competitors—a place where premium technology meets a genuinely welcoming community.

01

Vision

To make Johannesburg a recognised destination for inclusive, world-class esports.

02

Mission

To give every player access to premium technology, meaningful competition and community.

03

Goals

Grow local talent, host unforgettable events and create pathways into professional play.
